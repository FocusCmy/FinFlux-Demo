# Retrieve Research Context

Read only the selected ResearchItem count, Provider registry references, manifest SHA256 and bundle SHA256 supplied by the CaseEnvelope. Do not browse for replacement facts during the Run. Return `EMPTY` when no item is selected; do not synthesize a research record.
